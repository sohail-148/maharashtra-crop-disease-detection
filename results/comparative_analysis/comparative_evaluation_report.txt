5. RESEARCH FINDINGS & KEY OBSERVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Grape Discrepancies:
   - G1 (Niphad) achieves high accuracy driven by well-separated Downy Mildew and Healthy Leaves.
   - G2 (2024) performance is tempered by Leaf Blight (F1 ~0.59) and Esca (F1 ~0.74), which exhibit
     similar brown necrotic foliage lesions causing mutual confusion.

2. Sugarcane Discrepancies:
   - S1 (Maharashtra) and S2 (Large) both demonstrate strong alignment on shared classes (Healthy,
     Mosaic, Yellow Leaf).
   - In S2, distinctive physical pathologies such as Sett Rot (F1 0.99) and Grassy Shoot (F1 0.96)
     achieve near-perfect classification, whereas Smut (F1 0.65) shows the highest error rate.

3. Methodological Note:
   - Differences in observed performance between G1/G2 and S1/S2 represent observed source-domain
     and disease-taxonomy characteristics rather than conclusive domain shifts, providing
     empirically defensible insights for Maharashtra agricultural deployment.
