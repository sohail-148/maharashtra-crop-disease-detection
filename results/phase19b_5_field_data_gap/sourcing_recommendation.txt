1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & RESOLUTION OF AUDIT ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 19B-5 investigates the severe domain generalization failure observed on the 
Phase 18 clean multi-crop field benchmark (n=1,172):
  * Tomato Field Accuracy: 18.91% (87/460 correct; 87.6% collapse into Blights)
  * Grape Field Accuracy:  12.14% (34/280 correct; 74.3% collapse into Healthy/Powdery)

This revision resolves two critical factual issues identified during technical review:

A. FIELDPLANT TOMATO TAXONOMY CORRECTION:
   - The original FieldPlant publication (Moupojou et al., IEEE Access 2023) provides
     exactly 6 tomato categories:
       1. Tomato Bacterial Wilt
       2. Tomato Blight Leaf
       3. Tomato Brown Spots
       4. Tomato Healthy
       5. Tomato Leaf Mosaic Virus
       6. Tomato Leaf Yellow Virus
   - FieldPlant does NOT directly provide:
       * Spider Mites
       * Target Spot
       * Leaf Mold
       * Septoria Leaf Spot
       * Late Blight
   - Previous claims that FieldPlant supplies all 10 canonical classes are WITHDRAWN.
   - Biological mappings are strictly rejected:
       * 'Tomato Bacterial Wilt' (Ralstonia solanacearum) is biologically and symptomatically
         distinct from 'Bacterial Spot' (Xanthomonas perforans/euvesicatoria).
       * 'Tomato Brown Spots' is not verified as Target Spot (Corynespora cassiicola).
       * 'Tomato Blight Leaf' is generic and cannot be mapped to Early vs. Late Blight.
   - Strictly Legitimate FieldPlant Classes for Project Supplementation:
       1. Tomato Healthy (directly targets the 100% false-positive rate on healthy field foliage)
       2. Tomato Leaf Mosaic Virus (directly targets 0.0% field benchmark accuracy)
       3. Tomato Leaf Yellow Virus [TYLCV] (directly targets 7.1% field benchmark accuracy)

B. CRITICAL GVLiD IDENTITY VERIFICATION:
   - Local inspection of directory 'grape_2024' (Internal Dataset G2, n=3,477) confirms:
       * Total image count: exactly 3,477 files across 4 folders
       * Image dimensions: exactly 1080 x 1080 pixels
       * Classes: Black rot (808), esca (888), healthy (1109), leaf blight (672)
       * Filename prefixes: 'black rot001.jpg', 'esca001.jpg', 'healthy001.jpg', 'leaf blight001.jpg'
       * Split presence: already actively partitioned inside splits/grape_unified/train.csv
   - This local structure is 100% IDENTICAL to GVLiD (Shikalgar et al., 2024, Mendeley Data).
   - Finding: GVLiD IS ALREADY OUR INTERNAL G2 DATASET!
   - Action: GVLiD is marked as INTERNAL / ALREADY USED and removed from external acquisition.

C. GRAPE SOURCE AUDIT & HERMOS INCLUSION:
   - HERMOS (Özacar et al., 2024; Mendeley Data: 10.17632/j4xs3kh3fd.2; CC BY 4.0; n=914)
     is added as the primary external field candidate for Grape.
   - Captured in authentic commercial vineyards in Gediz River Basin, West Anatolia, Turkey.
   - Directly supplies: Powdery Mildew (0 in Track C, 3.3% acc), Downy Mildew (4.0% acc), Healthy.
   - Supplementary candidates retained: standalone ESCA-dataset (n=800) and Merlot Downy Mildew (n=99).
   - Targeted source for Grape Bacterial Leaf Spot (0 in all benchmarks): iNaturalist (CC-BY).
   - Excluded: FieldVitis (aggregates PlantDoc), PlantDoc (benchmark), GLDD (deferred, 140 in benchmarks),
     NGLD (internal G1), and Burmese Grape (botanical mismatch).
